How this was made

Search results were pulled with SerpAPI (Google web search restricted to reddit.com, open web search, and Google News) for queries about parking in Kozhikode, Kochi, the rest of Kerala and major Indian cities. Results were deduplicated by URL, filtered to items that describe a real parking problem, and tagged by city and problem type. The one-line summaries were written by an AI model from the search title and snippet only, so open the original before quoting it. Collected .

Reddit post bodies were not fetched: the cards show what the search engine showed. Counts reflect what the searches surfaced, not how common each problem is.
