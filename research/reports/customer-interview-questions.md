# Customer interview guide — 2026-10-23

1. How many vehicles operate in each market today, and what is the monthly fleet-growth plan?
2. Where do vehicles park between trips, charge, clean, inspect, and receive maintenance?
3. How many sites serve the fleet, and which are owned, leased, or partner-operated?
4. What are arrival, departure, queue, and peak retrieval distributions by hour and weekday?
5. What is the P50/P95 time from depot arrival to ready-for-service release?
6. How much floor area and how many positions are available, and what resource is constrained?
7. What are charger count, power, connector types, utilization, fault rate, and queue time?
8. What vehicle energy use and state-of-charge threshold trigger depot return or charging?
9. What are existing electrical capacity, service-upgrade scope, tariff, demand charges, and interconnection timeline?
10. How many labor hours per vehicle are spent charging, cleaning, inspecting, repositioning, and handing off service?
11. What are deadhead miles, off-service hours, charging downtime, and utilization by market?
12. Which dispatch, telematics, vehicle-state, charger, and work-order interfaces can an approved vendor use?
13. What vehicle geometry, clearance, weight, charge-port, and emergency-egress constraints govern mechanical storage?
14. What fail-safe mode, manual bypass, emergency retrieval, fire isolation, and recovery times are required?
15. Which parcels, leases, zoning, access, and expansion options are actually available for fleet use?
16. Which adopted building/fire code, conditional-use review, fire access, and emergency procedures apply?
17. Who owns capex, uptime, vehicle-damage liability, data, insurance, warranties, and permits?
18. What contract restrictions govern site design, vehicle movement, data, and third-party equipment?
19. What independently measured capacity, throughput, retrieval distribution, uptime, service burden, certification, and installed price can parking suppliers provide?
20. Would an anonymized shadow-mode simulation be useful, and what measured threshold would justify a physical pilot?

The questions are also in `research/data/normalized/interview_questions.csv`; none has an answer yet.
