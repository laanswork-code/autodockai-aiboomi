# Investor narrative — 2026-10-23

**Verified facts:** commercial robotaxi fleets are creating staffed depot operations. Lyft/Flexdrive publicly describes an 80,000-square-foot Nashville AV depot with fast charging, maintenance, parts storage, and more than 70 jobs; Waymo uses external partners for fleet/depot operations in Nashville and Dallas. Existing automated parking vendors also have US installations. [OPS-01](https://www.lyft.com/blog/posts/flexdrive-nashville-av-depot), [OPS-02](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/04/your-waymo-ride-now-arriving-in-nashville/), [OPS-03](https://waymo.com/blog/2025/07/our-next-city-dallas/), [COMP-01](https://parkplusinc.com/projects/)

**AutoDock hypothesis:** combining compact robotic storage, charging, and fleet orchestration could increase usable depot capacity or reduce the time and labor needed to return vehicles to service.

**Analyst inference:** depot workflow complexity and expansion justify interviews. Existing fleet managers, charging networks, and parking-system suppliers could become partners or competitors. Public sources do not yet show that AutoDock's specific combination lowers cost or improves availability.

**Projection status:** no dollar TAM, payback, customer commitment, deployment result, signed MOU, or US operating site is substantiated. The next fundable milestone is a customer-backed shadow-mode study with measured workflow and site data, followed by a safety, integration, and cost gate.
