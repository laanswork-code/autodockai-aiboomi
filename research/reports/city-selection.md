# City selection — 2026-10-23

| City | Commercial/deployment signal | Infrastructure/permit signal | Readout |
|---|---|---|---|
| San Francisco | Waymo service and a Zoox Explorers program; Lucid/Nuro/Uber announced a planned Bay Area program. | CA DMV and CPUC processes are distinct. A San Francisco record discusses conditional-use review for fleet charging in specified PDR districts. | High operator signal, but parcel/zoning and procurement risk are material. No site availability or current rent benchmark verified. |
| Los Angeles | Waymo service; Terawatt reports an LA fleet-charging site; vendors list automated-parking projects. | California permits plus site-specific utility, land-use, and building review. | Attractive partner discovery location; an existing charging site is not evidence of an available robotaxi depot. |
| Phoenix | Waymo service and dated Waymo fleet disclosure across Phoenix/LA/SF. | ADOT requires a law-enforcement interaction plan and written statements for driverless operation. | Useful operator comparison; depot, utility, and parcel facts remain unknown. |
| Austin | Waymo and Tesla service signals. | Texas commercial AV authorization became enforceable May 28, 2026; emergency-services plan is part of the process. | Highest variety among the four target cities, but no public depot footprint or cost comparison. |
| Nashville (additional) | Waymo public service and Flexdrive's new AV depot. | Site-specific local approvals and utility headroom were not researched. | Strongest first operating-workflow interview lead; not a confirmed physical pilot site. |
| Las Vegas (additional) | Zoox public service and Motional/Uber service; Waymo expansion signals. | Nevada/local site requirements and parcel data not researched. | Strong multi-operator interview market; depot ownership and site access unknown. |

Sources: [Waymo current service updates](https://waymo.com/blog/), [Zoox Las Vegas](https://zoox.com/journal/las-vegas), [Motional/Uber Las Vegas](https://investor.uber.com/news-events/news/press-release-details/2026/Uber-and-Motional-Launch-Robotaxi-Service-in-Las-Vegas/), [California DMV](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/vehicle-industry-services/autonomous-vehicles/autonomous-vehicle-testing-permit-holders/), [California CPUC](https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/regulatory-services/licensing/transportation-licensing-and-analysis-branch/autonomous-vehicle-programs/phase-i-driverless-autonomous-vehicle-deployment-program-advice-letter-status), [TxDMV](https://www.txdmv.gov/AVprogram), [ADOT](https://azdot.gov/autonomous-vehicles-testing-and-operating-without-driver), [San Francisco record](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/Item_6_Appeal_No._24-002_at_140_14th_Street.pdf), [Nashville depot](https://www.lyft.com/blog/posts/flexdrive-nashville-av-depot).

No city is selected for a physical pilot. Nashville ranks first for discovery because an operator-partner depot is public. Austin and Las Vegas are good multi-operator follow-ups. Site availability, rent, rates, utility capacity, labor, and permitting costs remain unscored.
