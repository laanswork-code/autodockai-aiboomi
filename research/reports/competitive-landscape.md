# Competitive landscape — 2026-10-23

## Direct parking systems

- **ParkPlus** lists automated US installations, including a 411-space Miami system with 80 charging-capable spaces. It is established in residential and commercial projects; no robotaxi orchestration deployment or public pricing was found. [COMP-01](https://parkplusinc.com/projects/)
- **Lödige** reports automated parking work in North American cities including New York and Boston. [COMP-02](https://www.lodige.com/en-us/company/where-we-work/america-lodige-usa/)
- **WÖHR** offers automated and mechanical systems, including EV-charging configurations; project and US robotaxi evidence is not established here. [COMP-03](https://woehr.de/en/virtual-city/automatic-parksystems.html)
- **AUTOParkit** markets robotic parking garages and publishes capacity/operating claims that require independent validation. [COMP-04](https://autoparkit.com/)

## Depot, charging, and fleet-service substitutes

- **Flexdrive/Lyft** and **Avis** already provide fleet operations, readiness, maintenance, infrastructure, or depot services for Waymo. These are direct substitutes for parts of AutoDock OS and operations/service. [OPS-01](https://www.lyft.com/blog/posts/flexdrive-nashville-av-depot), [OPS-03](https://waymo.com/blog/2025/07/our-next-city-dallas/)
- **Terawatt** markets charging sites and operations for autonomous/rideshare fleets. It could become a site/channel partner or compete for the same infrastructure layer. [INFRA-01](https://www.terawattinfrastructure.com/solutions/autonomous-and-rideshare-fleets)
- **Prologis Mobility** combines logistics real estate with fleet charging and mobility services; current evidence is mainly commercial trucking. [INFRA-03](https://www.prologis.com/fleet-charging-for-shippers)
- **Joule Labs** markets robotaxi charging and depot orchestration; public deployment proof and pricing were not independently verified. [COMP-05](https://www.joulelabs.com/robotaxi-charging-infrastructure)

## Gap and implication

This search did not verify a US deployment that combines automated vehicle storage, charging, and robotaxi fleet orchestration. That is a research gap, not proof of market absence. AutoDock's differentiation must be tested against existing mechanical parking suppliers, charger/energy platforms, and fleet operators that already run conventional depots. No public price data allows a cost comparison.
