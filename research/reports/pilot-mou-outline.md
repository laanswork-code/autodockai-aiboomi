# Non-binding pilot MOU outline — 2026-10-23

**Status: draft outline only. No counterpart has been contacted and no MOU exists.**

## Proposed scope

- Phase 1: shadow-mode analysis of anonymized depot arrival, staging, charging, maintenance, and release events.
- Phase 2: simulated parking/charging schedules and operator review of results.
- Physical equipment is excluded until a separate safety, site, utility, permitting, insurance, and commercial approval.

## Proposed measures

P50/P95 arrival-to-ready time; retrieval time; charging queue and utilization; labor touchpoints; usable positions/area; exception and recovery rate; energy cost per ready vehicle. Baseline and target values remain blank until the customer provides data.

## Terms requiring agreement

1. Parties, authorized signatories, site, and pilot dates.
2. Data scope, anonymization, security, retention, permitted use, and deletion.
3. Site access, system boundaries, vehicle permissions, and safety responsibilities.
4. Hardware ownership, installation, maintenance, and removal if a later physical phase is approved.
5. Fees, expenses, capex, utility costs, and payment milestones.
6. Insurance, warranties, indemnity, liability cap, incident response, and IP ownership.
7. Success measures, baseline, data-quality rules, and acceptance procedure.
8. Confidentiality, publicity, publication rights, and use of customer name/logo.
9. Termination rights and treatment of work in progress.
10. Governing law, dispute process, and non-binding/binding sections.

This outline is not legal advice and should be reviewed by both parties' counsel. No customer-specific terms or acceptance targets have been invented.
