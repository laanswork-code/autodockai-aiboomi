# Go-to-market workspace — 2026-10-23

## First-customer hypothesis

Start discovery with an **AV fleet-services operator that already runs a multi-vehicle depot**. Flexdrive Nashville is the clearest public lead because Lyft describes a new depot with fast charging, maintenance, parts, and staffing, and Waymo identifies Flexdrive as its fleet-management partner. This is not evidence that Flexdrive has a capacity problem or wants automated parking. [OPS-01](https://www.lyft.com/blog/posts/flexdrive-nashville-av-depot), [OPS-02](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/04/your-waymo-ride-now-arriving-in-nashville/)

## Recommended sequence

1. **Lyft/Flexdrive:** ask for an operations interview on vehicle arrival, staging, charging, maintenance, and retrieval. Public rationale: newly opened Nashville depot. No message sent.
2. **Terawatt:** validate whether its autonomous/rideshare customers need dense vehicle storage and whether Terawatt would partner or compete. No message sent. [INFRA-01](https://www.terawattinfrastructure.com/solutions/autonomous-and-rideshare-fleets)
3. **Avis:** understand the fleet-service and depot role disclosed in the Waymo Dallas partnership. No message sent. [OPS-03](https://waymo.com/blog/2025/07/our-next-city-dallas/)
4. **Waymo:** interview only through an official company channel or partner referral; seek operator metrics and integration constraints.

## Pilot ask

Ask for a paid or sponsored **shadow-mode study** using anonymized depot event data. Compare current staging with a simulated slotting and charging schedule. Proposed measures: P50/P95 arrival-to-ready time, retrieval time, charger queue/utilization, labor touchpoints, space utilization, exceptions, and energy cost per ready vehicle. Require an operator sponsor, data agreement, baseline period, and explicit success threshold before modeling outcomes.

Only consider a physical pilot after customer/site permission, electrical and land-use checks, vehicle/OEM interface approval, fire/building review, certification, insurance, and a validated economic case. AutoDock's site claims remain unverified company claims.
