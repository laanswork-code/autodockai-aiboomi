# Executive summary — 2026-10-23

## Thesis verdict

**Depot operations are a real operating need; the case for AutoDock's combined robotic-storage, charging, and orchestration product is still unproven.** The strongest public evidence is Lyft/Flexdrive's new 80,000-square-foot Nashville AV depot for Waymo. Lyft describes dozens of fast chargers sized to support hundreds of AVs, maintenance bays, secure parts storage, and more than 70 full-time roles. This confirms a meaningful, staffed depot operation. It does not show that automated parking would improve its cost or vehicle availability. [OPS-01](https://www.lyft.com/blog/posts/flexdrive-nashville-av-depot), [OPS-02](https://waymo.com/blog/2026/04/your-waymo-ride-now-arriving-in-nashville/)

Waymo has also named Avis as a multi-year partner for fleet infrastructure, readiness, maintenance, and depot operations in Dallas. This is evidence that operators can buy or partner for depot operations, and evidence of an incumbent substitute. [OPS-03](https://waymo.com/blog/2025/07/our-next-city-dallas/)

## Strongest support

- Fleet services now involve charging, maintenance, parts, staging, and dedicated labor at a public AV depot. [OPS-01](https://www.lyft.com/blog/posts/flexdrive-nashville-av-depot)
- Robotaxi service is expanding across multiple operators and cities. Waymo's current update page reports service in more than ten US cities; Zoox and Motional/Uber have public Las Vegas service signals. [OPS-04](https://waymo.com/blog/), [OPS-05](https://zoox.com/journal/las-vegas), [OPS-07](https://investor.uber.com/news-events/news/press-release-details/2026/Uber-and-Motional-Launch-Robotaxi-Service-in-Las-Vegas/)
- Existing automated parking vendors report US installations, including a vendor-listed 411-space Miami system with 80 charging-capable spaces. That demonstrates adjacent component supply, not a robotaxi deployment. [COMP-01](https://parkplusinc.com/projects/)

## Strongest challenge and uncertainty

- Fleet managers already provide conventional depot operations through Flexdrive and Avis. The customer may value a partner-run depot more than a new mechanical system. [OPS-01](https://www.lyft.com/blog/posts/flexdrive-nashville-av-depot), [OPS-03](https://waymo.com/blog/2025/07/our-next-city-dallas/)
- No public robotaxi-depot data was found for occupancy, charger utilization, empty miles, charging dwell, labor cost per vehicle, retrieval distribution, or depot capex.
- No independent US evidence was found for AutoDock deployment, customer use, vehicle integration, measured capacity, retrieval, safety, or a contract. AutoDock's own terms say its products are in development and specifications/timelines are indicative. [AD-01](https://autodockai.com/home), [AD-03](https://autodockai.com/terms)
- WGI's 2026 $33,300-per-space median is a conventional parking-structure benchmark, not a depot or tower cost. [ECON-01](https://wginc.com/blog/2026-zweig-group-marketing-excellence-awards)

## Recommendation

Run customer discovery before publishing a dollar TAM or proposing a physical installation. Start with Flexdrive Nashville, then Terawatt and Avis. Ask for anonymized workflow data and a shadow-mode study. Do not position tower capacity, robotic charging, or retrieval performance as validated. No outreach was sent and no pilot or MOU exists.
