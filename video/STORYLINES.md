# Storylines

Picked: **A. One morning at KSUM**, because a stranger follows a driver with a nearly empty battery more easily than a diagram, and every beat can be the real product.

## A. One morning at KSUM (picked)

- **Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** object-to-object chain through the real UI.
- A driver with 4% battery circles a full parking strip at the Kerala Startup Mission. The camera follows the problem out to 474 real complaints, then back into the digital twin, where two towers replace four bays and Jev decides each car's morning, asks a person when it is unsure, and keeps the grid calm.
- **First two seconds:** the real twin in Today mode, the full strip, the clock line "9:40 am" landing on it.
- **Beats:** hook (Kerala and India, complaints scroll) → tension (474 complaints, the /problems page) → reveal (KSUM as one example, a live digital twin; 40 cars on 4 bays) → why Jev (one request, five answers) → how (confident acts, unsure asks: the camera misread) → proof (dashboard, simulated rush hour) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the app's own: paper ground #f3efe6, ink #16181b, sign blue #1747a6 rationed to the payoff, road-marking yellow stripe, Inter for type.
- **Sound:** narrated (ElevenLabs, Alice); a calm minimal bed that opens when the towers appear; a pop on numbers landing, whooshes only on world changes.

## B. The number lands

- **Device:** a number lands. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds.
- 474 becomes 15 bays becomes 40 cars becomes 29% less grid peak, each number earned by a real screen and then made physical as giant type.
- **First two seconds:** "474" filling the frame, built from the problem cards.
- **Beats:** Kerala and India hook → 474 complaints → KSUM as one example, a live digital twin → 4 bays, 40 cars → one request, five answers → 29% lower grid peak → claim → end card.
- **Look:** giant Inter numerals on paper, ink and one blue.
- **Sound:** music-led, cut on the bar, no voice.

## C. The object is the explanation

- **Device:** the object is the explanation. **Form:** a flight through one world.
- The camera enters the rotary tower and travels with one car through the pipeline: gate camera, event bus, Jev, orchestrator, pallet, charger, phone.
- **First two seconds:** a car's number plate filling the frame at the gate.
- **Beats:** plate → event → Jev's five answers → pallet to door → charging → payment → end card.
- **Look:** the 3D twin as the stage, labels as road signs.
- **Sound:** narrated, mechanical effects on the tower moving.
