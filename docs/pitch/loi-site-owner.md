## The problem at your site

Your site is the first example we have modelled of a problem found across Kerala and India: of 474 public parking complaints in our research, 154 come from Kerala. Here, the paver strip in front of the building holds about 15 cars, is full by mid-morning, and has no EV charging. Visitors circle, park on the roadside, or leave.

## What we propose

Two AutoDock rotary towers on four of today's parking bays (about 57 m²). Together they hold 40 cars, and every bay has an AC charger. Our decision layer runs the towers: which pallet each car goes to, when it charges, the price, and checks on plate reads and payments, with staff approving anything it is unsure about. The other 11 surface bays stay in use.

We have modelled this on your actual site in 3D. Our simulation (labelled as such) compares today's layout with the towers on waiting time, kWh delivered, revenue per bay and grid peak.

## Pilot outline

1. **Site survey (2 weeks):** ground, power supply, access and approvals, with your facilities team.
2. **Proposal:** a costed plan for installation by AutoDock AI and operation by our layer, including revenue share options (per-session fee, energy margin, landowner share).
3. **Decision:** you decide whether to proceed under a separate agreement.

## Terms

- This letter is **non-binding**. It creates no obligation to host, pay for, or approve any installation.
- Site information shared with us is used only for this proposal and kept confidential.
- Either party may end these discussions at any time by email.
