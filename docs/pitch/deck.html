Across Kerala and India, cars and EVs outgrow parking and chargers, and cities have no spare land.

154 in Kerala (Kochi 55, Thiruvananthapuram 38, Kozhikode 22), 320 in the rest of India (Delhi NCR 55, Mumbai 43, Bengaluru 37). From Reddit, forums and news: fights over spots, EV charging, apartments, fees.

Insight · why now

The tower exists.
The brain doesn't.

Hardware is solved

AutoDock AI (Kozhikode) already builds rotary towers with a charger in every bay. In our model a 20-pallet tower stands on 2 parking bays.

Every car is a set of decisions

Which pallet, charge now (on solar, cheaper by day) or wait for off-peak, what price, is this the right car and is it paid for. Rules get these wrong at the edges; people can't watch every car.

Why now: typed, calibrated AI

TypeSafe's Jev returns typed answers with a calibrated confidence in about half a second, for about $0.00008 a decision. Cheap enough to decide on every event, and honest enough to ask a person when it isn't sure.

Why now: the grid

More EVs than sockets, and the site's grid connection is the bottleneck. Robotaxi fleets are scaling in the US, and need depots that run unattended.

Solution · live demo

Jev decides.
It asks when unsure.

  • 3D twin of one real example site, KSUM Kalamassery: two towers on 4 of 15 bays, 40 cars on 57 m².
  • Per car: bay, charge plan, price, plate and payment checks, each with Jev's confidence.
  • Unsure charge or anomaly calls go to the operator queue. Here: a camera misread Jev wouldn't guess on.
  • Solar and grid flows, live kW gauge, Jev cost counter, event log.
  • Driver phone: arrive, dock, charge, pay by UPI (mock).

Five judgments, one call

  • Bay: Choice over 4-6 pallets code proposes
  • Charge: Choice (now, off-peak, none)
  • Price: Score, demand 0-3, mapped to a tier
  • Plate, payment: two Nouls (probabilities)

Confidence counts options with the same outcome together: indifference between equivalent pallets is not doubt.

Guardrails

  • Rules fallback on any error or timeout, same interface
  • Flagged plate or payment always reaches a person
  • Audit log of every decision and its confidence
  • 60 tests; seeded simulator for rules vs Jev

Through AutoDock AI as the hardware partner. First a Kerala pilot site (KSUM / KINFRA style campuses), then Kochi, Trivandrum and Kozhikode; fleets and robotaxi depots next.

Roadmap · risks

From simulation
to a real tower.

Next

  • 4 weeks: integrate with AutoDock's tower controller on recorded events.
  • 8 weeks: shadow pilot at one Kerala site: Jev logs what it would decide, controls nothing.
  • Calibrate thresholds on real outcomes; tune for each site's grid and tariffs.
  • Live pilot under a separate agreement if the shadow results hold.

Risks, stated plainly

  • Our numbers are simulated; real behaviour may differ.
  • Depends on a hardware partner and site approvals (grid, fire, structural).
  • Jev's calibration checked only on synthetic cases so far.
  • Demo 3D models come from AutoDock's demo; permission needed for public use.

The ask: a pilot site, an introduction to AutoDock AI's team, and mentors who have run EV or parking operations.
